READING THE RESULT

A sample description,
not a population estimate.

The pathway chart uses all in-school records in the released SWTS file. Ethnicity codes 1 and 2 are combined as Bumiputera, while detailed school types are grouped into four pathway families.

The salary chart uses valid maximum offers from employer records where A1 = 3. These are reported offers, not realised earnings or causal returns to qualifications.

KRI's published report used survey weights, but no identifiable weight field is present in the public CSVs. Differences from published estimates are therefore expected.
